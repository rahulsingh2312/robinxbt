## Notes before you post

Keep the automated-account label in the profile exactly as it is — the voice
only works as parody while it is clearly labelled as one, and that label is
also what keeps the account alive.

None of this copy promises a return, claims Robinhood endorsement, or says the
token does anything. That is deliberate. "i deployed a token and i have an
attitude" is a narrative; "this will go up" is a liability, and it is also the
least funny sentence available.
